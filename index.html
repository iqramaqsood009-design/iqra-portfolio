About

Ideas, carried through to delivery.

I’m Iqra Maqsood, a Media Producer and Television Director at Discover Pakistan TV in Lahore, where I have worked since August 2023. Over 3+ years I have produced and directed television shows, documentaries, special features and promotional content.

I take projects from the first idea to final delivery: concept development, research and scripting, shoot planning, studio and on-location direction, interviews, and close work with presenters, camera operators and editors. My documentaries cover Pakistan’s culture, regional heritage, tourism, craftsmanship, sports industry and wildlife.

I also work with AI tools such as Google Flow, ElevenLabs and CapCut to create fashion films, short stories, ads and show teasers, and I completed a course in Artificial Intelligence at KIPS in 2026.
